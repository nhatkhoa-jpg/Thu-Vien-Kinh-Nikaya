# Theravāda library scope

Confirmed product rule — 2026-09-02.

## Public languages
Keep the focused public UI set used by the established product:

- `vi` — Vietnamese
- `th` — Thai
- `my` — Burmese
- `si` — Sinhala
- `km` — Khmer
- `lo` — Lao
- `en` — English
- `zh` — Chinese

Do not expand the public selector merely to maximize language count. Other locales remain deferred/noindex and may be re-enabled only when there is a concrete audience, source-backed scripture coverage, and QA capacity. When a public locale lacks a local scripture translation, English may be offered as an **explicitly labelled temporary fallback**; never silently present English as if it were the selected-language translation.

This is a product/audience rule, not a claim about any individual's beliefs.

## Canon scope
The library is expanding from the five Nikāyas toward a broader Theravāda Pāli Canon library:

1. **Sutta Piṭaka** — five Nikāyas remain the primary reading corpus.
2. **Vinaya Piṭaka** — Bhikkhu-vibhaṅga, Bhikkhunī-vibhaṅga, Mahāvagga, Cūḷavagga, and Parivāra.
3. **Abhidhamma Piṭaka** — Dhammasaṅgaṇī, Vibhaṅga, Dhātukathā, Puggalapaññatti, Kathāvatthu, Yamaka, and Paṭṭhāna.
4. Important Khuddaka works should be discoverable and progressively materialized, including Dhammapada, Sutta Nipāta, Udāna, Itivuttaka, Theragāthā, Therīgāthā, Jātaka, Buddhavaṁsa, and Cariyāpiṭaka.

## Source and rights
- Do not ingest scripture from screenshots, Studocu, scraped reposts, or aggregators without provenance and rights.
- Prefer canonical Pāli identity and stable source references.
- Vietnamese translations associated with respected translators, including Đại Trưởng Lão Tịnh Sự, may be prioritized for source research, but full-text mirroring requires verified redistribution permission/license or another lawful source.
- AI may assist metadata/UI/search, but must never invent scripture text or present an AI translation as an official source translation.
