# AdSense readiness

The library is being prepared for monetization without making advertising part of the scripture-reading experience.

## Implemented in the site

- Clear global navigation and a persistent footer.
- About, Privacy, Terms, and Sources & Editorial Policy pages.
- Source attribution and a no-invented-scripture editorial rule.
- Sitemap coverage for trust/policy pages.
- Responsive mobile/desktop reading and audio controls.
- Incomplete language interfaces remain noindex until their corpus is mature; English may be used as a transparent full-text fallback.

## Before enabling AdSense

1. Add the real AdSense publisher ID only after the site is accepted. Do not publish a placeholder `ads.txt` entry.
2. Configure a Google-certified CMP / Privacy & messaging flow for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland if personalized ads are served there.
3. Update the Privacy Policy with the exact analytics, advertising and consent providers that are actually enabled.
4. Keep ads away from primary reading controls, audio controls, download buttons and navigation so clicks cannot be confused with content actions.
5. Prefer substantial reader pages and source-rich editorial material over thin catalogue-only landing pages.
6. Re-run mobile, accessibility, performance and broken-link checks after ad code is introduced.

## Product principle

Monetization must remain secondary to reading quality, source transparency and preservation. The library should be fully usable even when advertising is unavailable or blocked.
